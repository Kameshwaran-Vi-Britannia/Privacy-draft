Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of personal data being processed and entities it was shared with.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Obligation on platforms to delete your data when consent is withdrawn.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India if issues aren't resolved.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise data rights in case of death or incapacity.

Emergency Incident Response Playbooks

Step-by-step emergency instructions for cyber incidents and security breaches.

Financial Fraud / UPI Scam
  1. Immediately call national helpline 1930 (National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal).
  2. Block your UPI APPs, SIM, and bank cards via net banking or customer care.
  3. Lodge an official report at cybercrime.gov.in within 2 hours ("Golden Hour").
  4. Submit formal written complaint to bank branch manager to invoke zero-liability norms.
Account Takeover / Hacked Account
  1. Attempt password reset via linked recovery phone/email.
  2. Revoke active sessions/devices in Google, Meta, or WhatsApp security settings.
  3. Inform contacts on other platforms that your account is compromised to prevent scamming.
  4. Report account compromise directly to platform emergency support desk.
Lost or Stolen Smartphone
  1. Visit CEIR Portal (Govt of India) to block device IMEI network-wide.
  2. Remotely wipe device via Google Find My Device or Apple iCloud.
  3. Block your SIM card immediately via telecom provider to stop OTP interception.
  4. File an e-FIR at local police portal for lost property.
