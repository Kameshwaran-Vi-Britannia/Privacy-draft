My Personal Data Privacy Checklist

For college students and club volunteers. Tick what you already do. The score updates itself.

If something goes wrong: money lost in online fraud, call 1930 and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Account hacked, change the password, log out of all devices and turn on 2FA. Cyber incidents can be reported to CERT-In (cert-in.org.in). Company misused your data, complain to the company first, then the Data Protection Board of India.

Many DPDP Act obligations for companies start in phases until about May 2027. Check MeitY for the latest dates. This checklist is for education and is not legal advice.
