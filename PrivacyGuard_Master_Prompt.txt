ORIGINAL CHECKLIST: EXACTLY 8 CATEGORIES AND 47 ITEMS
Preserve the wording and category distinctions below.

CATEGORY 1 — Apps and permissions (6)
1. I check the permission list before installing an app.
2. I deny permissions an app does not need (a torch app asking for contacts or mic).
3. I set location to "Only while using the app" or "Never" for most apps.
4. I download apps only from the official Play Store or App Store.
5. I delete apps I no longer use and review permissions every month.
6. I read the privacy notice for what data is collected and why.

CATEGORY 2 — Passwords and accounts (6)
1. Each important account (email, banking, college portal) has a different password.
2. My passwords are long (12+ characters), not names or birthdays.
3. I use two-step verification (2FA) on email and social media.
4. I never share OTPs, PINs or passwords, even with "bank staff" or "college office".
5. I log out of shared or lab computers.
6. I use a password manager or another safe way to remember passwords.

CATEGORY 3 — Social media and sharing (5)
1. My profile is private or has limited visibility.
2. I do not post my phone number, address, college ID, Aadhaar or boarding pass.
3. I avoid sharing live location and travel plans publicly.
4. I ask friends before tagging them or posting their photos.
5. I view my profile as a stranger would, to check what is visible.

CATEGORY 4 — Phishing and scam safety (5)
1. I do not click unknown links in SMS, WhatsApp, email or Instagram DMs.
2. I am careful with free internships, scholarships, prizes and KYC update messages.
3. I check the sender address or website carefully before entering details.
4. I never scan a QR code or approve a UPI request to "receive" money.
5. I verify unexpected requests by calling a known number.

CATEGORY 5 — Devices and Wi-Fi (5)
1. My phone and laptop have a screen lock.
2. I keep my phone, laptop and apps updated.
3. I avoid banking and important logins on public Wi-Fi.
4. I turn off Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when not in use.
5. I back up important files in a safe place.

CATEGORY 6 — Consent forms and club data (8)
Description: For volunteers who collect names, phone numbers or IDs for events.
1. I collect only the data really needed (name and email, not date of birth or address).
2. I tell people why I collect the data and how it will be used.
3. I get a clear "yes" and do not use pre-ticked boxes.
4. I store data safely with limited access, not in an open group chat.
5. I do not share contact lists with sponsors or outsiders without permission.
6. I delete the data after the event unless people agreed to keep it.
7. I let people correct or delete their data if they ask.
8. I get a parent or guardian's permission before collecting data of anyone under 18.

CATEGORY 7 — Using AI tools safely (5)
1. I never paste personal data (mine or others') into AI tools: names, phone numbers, ID numbers, marks, private chats.
2. I replace real details with fake ones ("Student A") in prompts.
3. I check my AI tool's privacy and chat-history settings.
4. I verify AI answers on law, health and money with an official source.
5. I do not treat AI answers as legal advice.

CATEGORY 8 — Know your data rights (DPDP Act, 2023) (7)
1. I know I am a Data Principal: the data belongs to me.
2. I know I can ask what data a company holds about me and how it is used.
3. I know I can ask to correct wrong data or erase data that is no longer needed.
4. I know I can withdraw consent at any time.
5. I know I can complain to the company first, then to the Data Protection Board of India.
6. I know I can nominate someone to act for me if I cannot.
7. I know I must also be honest: no fake identity and no false complaints.
