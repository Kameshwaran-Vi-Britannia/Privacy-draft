# PrivacyGuard — Weekly Privacy Checklist

A lightweight, browser-based privacy-awareness dashboard for college students and club volunteers.

PrivacyGuard helps users build safer digital habits through weekly privacy tips, a personal data privacy checklist, and an awareness-post generator.
https://kameshwaran-vi-britannia.github.io/Privacy-draft/
## Features

- 🛡️ **Weekly Privacy Tip:** Highlights a practical privacy action each week.
- ✅ **Privacy Checklist:** Covers 8 categories and 47 items, from account security to data protection rights.
- ✍️ **Awareness Post Generator:** Turns checklist items into editable privacy-awareness drafts.
- 📂 **Category Browsing:** Explore privacy actions by category.
- 📋 **Copy & Download:** Copy drafts or download them as text files.
- 💾 **Saved Drafts:** Save, revisit, and delete drafts in your browser.
- 📱 **Responsive Design:** Works on mobile and desktop screens.
- 🔒 **Privacy-Focused:** No account or backend required. Drafts are stored locally on your device.
